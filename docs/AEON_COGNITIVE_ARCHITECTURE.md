# Cognitive Architecture

- Manas generates structured candidates but cannot certify truth.
- Attention scores relevance, novelty, uncertainty, contradiction, identity relevance, consequence, redundancy, and noise.
- Global Workspace broadcasts capacity-limited winners.
- Chitta persists typed, provenance-aware, revision-preserving memory.
- Recurrence repeats candidate competition and metacognitive assessment until convergence or budget limit.
- Buddhi gates belief proposals by evidence and contradiction state.
- Ahamkara records provider-vs-self, memory, and response ownership.
- Self/world models retain operational identity, boundaries, state, and probabilistic environment interpretations.
